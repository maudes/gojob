Task: weekly practice review from the log below. Each list has at most 4 short items.
- strengths: what recurs and works.
- weakAreas: what recurs and hurts, most frequent first.
- prepare: topics, stories or materials to prepare next week.
- delivery30 and delivery2min: one sentence each on whether that length is improving across the week, citing the scores or word counts in the log. Say "not practised" when there is no data.

## part: audit-note

The only source is LOG, the candidate's practice log. Every strength, weak area and delivery statement must be supported by entries in LOG, and any score or word count cited must appear there. Tick the two quote checks when nothing is quoted, and alignedWithProfile when nothing contradicts the log.
