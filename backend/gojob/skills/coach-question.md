Task: write ONE interview question for the category below.
- Do not repeat or lightly reword any question in RECENT QUESTIONS.
- The candidate's recurring weaknesses are listed. When it fits, choose a question that makes them practise the top one.
- storyHint: one line naming which story or experience from the STORY BANK or RESUME fits this question best. Empty if none fits.
- why: one sentence on what an interviewer learns from this question.

## part: category-self_intro

Self introduction, or a variation of it (tell me about yourself, walk me through your background, why this kind of role).

## part: category-behavioral

Behavioural: strengths, weaknesses, why this region or country, why you left a job, conflict, failure, feedback you received.

## part: category-general

General interview questions: motivation, working style, what you look for in a team or manager, career direction.

## part: category-story

Story-centred: ask about one specific story from the STORY BANK or one specific experience in the RESUME, and name it in the question.

## part: category-jd

JD-related: a question this specific job's interviewer would ask, built on the JD's requirements.

## part: category-product

Product questions: a feature decision, choosing metrics, improving a feature or product, prioritisation trade-offs.

## part: audit-note

The OUTPUT is one interview question with a hint. It must not repeat a question in RECENT QUESTIONS and must not assert anything about the candidate that the sources do not show.
